Transitioning from Dhaka, Bangladesh, to Raleigh, North Carolina, marks the beginning of an exciting and challenging new chapter in my life. For years, my academic and professional daily routine revolved around the bustling campus of Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) and Sonargaon University, where I taught young, aspiring minds. The transition from teaching at a university podium in Dhaka to sitting as a PhD student at North Carolina State University (NCSU) has been both humbling and inspiring.

Raleigh welcomed me with its quiet, tree-lined streets, sprawling brick architecture, and friendly southern charm. While the fast-paced energy of Dhaka is something I will always carry with me, the serene academic environment at NC State offers a perfect backdrop for deep research in molecular dynamics. Adjusting to a new culture, navigating the campus, and setting up my research workstation has been an adventure. I look forward to sharing more reflections here as I balance coding Molecular Dynamics simulations with exploring life in North Carolina!
