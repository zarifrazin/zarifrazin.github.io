Childhood memories have a unique way of anchoring us, no matter how far we travel. Although I completed my engineering studies in Dhaka and have now moved to the United States, my thoughts frequently wander back to the village of Kalipur in the Chandpur district. Growing up on the tranquil banks of the magnificent Meghna River shaped my early understanding of peace and nature.

I fondly remember watching the wooden fishing boats glide across the vast, calm waters at sunset. The soft orange and pink gradients reflecting on the water surface were a daily masterpiece, far removed from the mechanical formulas and computer code I work with today. Sitting by the river bank, listening to the gentle lapping of the waves, was where I first learned to let my mind wander. Even now, during intense weeks of researching interfacial heat capacity at the nanoscale, looking back at photos of the Meghna River brings back that same deep sense of calm.
