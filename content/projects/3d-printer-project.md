### Project Overview
An end-to-end design and assembly of a custom Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) 3D printer. The project involved CAD design in SolidWorks, selecting appropriate stepper motors, controller boards, extruders, and heated beds, followed by hardware calibration and testing.

### Design and Hardware Specifications
- **Frame Layout**: Stiff aluminum extrusion construction to minimize structural vibration during high-speed printing.
- **Motion System**: CoreXY motion system for decoupled axis movements, leading to highly precise layer alignment.
- **Controller & Firmware**: Configured Marlin firmware on an 8-bit board, calibrating steps/mm for X, Y, Z axis, and extruder.
- **Extruder**: Direct drive extruder setup optimized for flexible filaments (TPU) and standard PLA/PETG.
