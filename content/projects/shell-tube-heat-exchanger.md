### Project Overview
This project presents the comprehensive design of an industrial Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger. The design process was split into two primary phases: Thermal Design (to determine surface area, fluid velocities, and pressure drops) and Mechanical Design (to satisfy shell thickness, tube sheet thickness, and flange requirements based on pressure parameters).

### Design Stages
1. **Thermal Design**:
   - Calculated Log Mean Temperature Difference (LMTD) and heat transfer coefficients using the Kern method.
   - Checked pressure drops on both shell and tube sides to meet safety specifications.
2. **Mechanical Design**:
   - Selected shell thickness, tube thickness, nozzle dimensions, and flange dimensions using standard stress calculations.
   - Drafted detailed mechanical engineering diagrams (CAD drawings) for manufacturing.
