Abstract Equilibrium Molecular Dynamics (EMD) simulations are carried out to investigate the effects of wall wettability and nanogap thickness (h) on the constant volume molar heat capacity (C v) of liquid argon entrapped between two solid copper walls at a fixed temperature of 100 K. Lennard-Jones (LJ) potential is employed to model the interaction between atoms, with nanogap thickness (h) varying between 0.585 nm to 5.85 nm. To characterize interfacial surface wettability as hydrophobic or hydrophilic, the solid-liquid interaction potential has been altered, and its effect on molar heat capacity (C v) has been evaluated. Simulation results reveal three key findings.
